WHERE IS THE SRI SUMERU GFRC & FRP MANUFACTURING FACILITY LOCATED?

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, featuring high-shear mixer stations, pneumatic spray-up rigs, precision CNC pattern making, and temperature-controlled curing yards.

WHAT QUALITY AND STRUCTURAL STANDARDS DO YOUR COMPOSITE ELEMENTS MEET?

All architectural elements comply with international PCI (Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute) and GRCA guidelines, delivering Grade 10P and Grade 18P strength with Characteristic MOR up to 18 N/mm².

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN GRADE 10P AND GRADE 18P GFRC?

Grade 10P is typically premix-cast for hollow decorative elements like brackets, balusters, and planters. Grade 18P utilizes multi-directional spray-up AR fibers for external facade cladding panels subject to high wind loads and seismic forces.

DO YOU PROVIDE STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING SHOP DRAWINGS AND WIND-LOAD CALCULATIONS?

Yes, our in-house engineering team provides complete shop drawings, connection engineering, finite element analysis (FEA), and wind-load verification for commercial and residential elevations.

HOW DOES SRI SUMERU ENSURE COLOR CONSISTENCY AND SURFACE TEXTURE FIDELITY?

We use UV-stable mineral oxide pigments, high-grade white Portland cement, and graded silica sands with automated batching to ensure exact color and texture consistency across entire building elevations.
